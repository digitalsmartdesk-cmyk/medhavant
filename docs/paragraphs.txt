
[Title] Medhavant: Top-Level Strategy
[Normal] Business Transformation Consulting for owner-run firms  |  Vikas Gupta  |  20 November 2026
[Heading 1] 1. The strategy in brief
[Normal] Medhavant will win by selling one-day AI programs that run on the owner’s own file and end with one number, then converting that number into paid diagnostics, pilots and monthly partnerships. Baroda Management Association (BMA) is the launch channel. The brand and website carry the wider business-transformation story to owners across India.

[Heading 1] 2. Why this is the right moment
[Normal] Owners of small and mid-sized firms are being told every week that AI will change their business, but few know where to start and fewer trust a vendor to start for them. Their daily reality is cash tied up with customers, rising costs, slow quotes and a week spent assembling numbers by hand.
[Normal] That gap is the opening. A consultant who skips the technology talk, uses the owner’s own files, and shows a concrete rupee or hour figure in one day lowers the risk of saying yes. The strategy below is built around that single move.
[Heading 1] 3. Positioning
[List Paragraph] Outcome first, AI second. Public material speaks of leaks found and time returned. AI appears as the method.
[List Paragraph] Strategic story on top, tactical programs below. The site opens with how Medhavant helps a business change in a shifting environment, then shows the program library.
[List Paragraph] Four capabilities. See clearly (numbers owners can trust), Steer daily (a rhythm of decisions), Grow (customers and margin), Govern (safe and accountable use of AI).
[List Paragraph] Plain wording. “Find the leak”, not “forensic audit”, which sounds like a fraud inquiry to an owner.

[Heading 1] 5. Offer architecture
[Normal] Each step is sold only after the previous one has produced a number. Fees are not yet set (see decisions in section 10).
[Normal] Suggested rule: the Clinic fee is credited against a Diagnostic booked within 30 days.
[Heading 2] The program library
[Normal] Find the leak: Cash stuck in market; Purchase price check; Rejection and rework cost; Expense leak scan; Penalty and clause scan.
[Normal] Get your time back: Monday sheet; Ask my Tally; Quote without me; Replies in my voice; Due-date watch.

[Heading 1] 7. Credibility without a client history
[List Paragraph] Number cards: rupees identified and hours a week returned, labelled as estimates until a pilot measures them.
[List Paragraph] Quotes and case notes only with written consent; anonymised unless the owner agrees to be named.
[List Paragraph] Until real proof exists, the website shows labelled potential use cases, two lines each, with no claim about past clients.
[List Paragraph] Authority from The AI Trilogy and from the IIM Ahmedabad background, used at the talk and in proposals.
